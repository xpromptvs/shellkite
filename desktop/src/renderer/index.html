Add a Mac

On the Mac you want to access:

  1. Open Shellkite and choose Access this Mac remotely.
  2. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility, then select Start sharing.
  3. Open the connection link on the device you want to use, or scan the QR code with a phone or tablet.

To see the Mac in your device list, sign in to Shellkite on both Macs with the same passkey.
